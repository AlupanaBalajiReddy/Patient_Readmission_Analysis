INTERPRETATION FRAMEWORK
======================================================================

1. Compare means and medians rather than relying only on means because several clinical activity variables are skewed.

2. A higher value in the early-readmission group indicates a higher observed level of that variable among encounters with early readmission.

3. These comparisons describe associations in the dataset and should not be interpreted as causal effects.
