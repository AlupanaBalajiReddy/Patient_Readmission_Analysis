INTERPRETATION FRAMEWORK
======================================================================

1. Compare each category's early-readmission rate with the overall dataset rate.

2. Categories with very small encounter counts should not be interpreted strongly because their observed rates can be unstable.

3. Administrative categories describe characteristics of the hospital encounter and its admission/discharge pathway.

4. Differences in readmission rates are descriptive associations and should not be interpreted as causal effects.
