======================================================================
INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
======================================================================

Categorical variables:
- Chi-square tests whether the distribution of early readmission differs across categories.
- Cramer's V describes the strength of association.

Numerical/count variables:
- Mann-Whitney U tests whether the distributions differ between early and non-early readmission groups.
- Rank-biserial correlation describes the magnitude and direction of group separation.

Multiple testing:
- Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate correction was applied separately to categorical and numerical/count tests.

Interpretation:
- An adjusted p-value below 0.05 indicates statistical evidence of an association under the selected test.
- Effect size should be considered alongside the p-value because large datasets can produce very small p-values for small differences.
- None of these tests establish causality.
