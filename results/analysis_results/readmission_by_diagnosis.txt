INTERPRETATION FRAMEWORK
======================================================================

1. Diagnosis codes were analyzed as categorical ICD-9 codes rather than numerical measurements.

2. Broad ICD-9 categories were used to reduce the number of individual diagnosis codes and improve interpretability.

3. Categories with very small encounter counts should not be interpreted strongly.

4. Major categories with at least 1,000 encounters provide a more stable descriptive comparison.

5. Differences in readmission rates are descriptive associations and should not be interpreted as causal effects.
