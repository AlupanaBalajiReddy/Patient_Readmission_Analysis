======================================================================
INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
======================================================================

Absolute percentage-point difference:
Difference between two observed early-readmission rates.

Relative difference:
Percentage increase or decrease relative to the reference group's observed rate.

Practical significance:
A statistically significant association may still have a small practical difference. Therefore, readmission rates and effect sizes should be considered together with p-values.

Caution:
These are encounter-level descriptive associations. They should not be interpreted as causal effects or individual patient risk estimates.
